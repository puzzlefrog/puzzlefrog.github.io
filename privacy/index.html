About this policy

This policy explains how PuzzleFrog Pty Ltd (ABN 31 625 507 404), “we” or “us”, handles personal information. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), and with the privacy obligations in our contracts with Australian Government agencies.

What we collect

Candidates and workers

  • name, contact details and date of birth
  • your CV, work history, qualifications, training and licences
  • referee details and what referees tell us
  • citizenship and right-to-work details
  • security clearance status and level
  • results of pre-placement checks, such as police checks
  • if you work with us: tax file number, bank, superannuation and emergency contact details, timesheets and performance feedback

Clients and business contacts

Your name, role, organisation and contact details, and details of the roles or work you ask us about.

Sensitive information

Some checks involve sensitive information, such as criminal history. We collect it only with your consent, and only when it is needed for a role you have agreed to be considered for.

Website visitors

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. See Our website.

How we collect it

  • directly from you, for example when you send a CV, email us, use our contact form, join our talent register or speak with us
  • from referees you nominate
  • from police check providers, with your consent
  • from a government organisation, when it asks us to employ someone it has already chosen
  • from professional profiles you have made public, such as LinkedIn

You can make a general enquiry without identifying yourself, but we can’t consider you for a role unless we know who you are.

How we use it

  • to work out which jobs suit you and, with your permission, put you forward for them
  • to complete the checks needed before you start a job
  • to employ or engage you, and pay you, your superannuation and your tax
  • to meet our legal, insurance and contract obligations, including agency security requirements
  • to answer enquiries and work with our clients
  • with your consent, to count you, without naming you, in the totals of available people we give in panel applications

We don’t use your information for direct marketing without your consent, and we never sell it.

Who we share it with

  • organisations you have agreed to be put forward to, including the results of your checks, with your consent
  • the Digital Transformation Agency (DTA), which runs the government panel we supply workers through, with your consent
  • the Australian Government Security Vetting Agency (AGSVA) and your sponsoring agency, for security clearances
  • police check providers, when we arrange your police check, with your consent
  • our payroll provider, superannuation funds and the Australian Taxation Office
  • our insurers and professional advisers
  • regulators and law enforcement, where the law requires or allows it

Overseas disclosure

We keep our email and documents, including CVs and check results, in Microsoft 365, which stores data for Australian businesses in Australia.

Some personal information is disclosed overseas, through our website:

  • Our website host, GitHub, Inc., is in the United States. It records visitors’ IP addresses.
  • Our contact form and talent register form are run by Web3Forms, a service operated from India using providers in the United States and other countries. Your answers pass through Web3Forms, and it keeps a copy.

We don’t ask for CVs, security clearance details or check results through our website forms. See Our website for more detail.

Storage and security

We store personal information electronically in Microsoft 365, with access limited to the people who need it.

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires. For example, employee records must be kept for seven years under the Fair Work Act 2009 (Cth). After that we securely destroy or de-identify it. If you’re on our talent register, we check every six months that you still want to be on it, and remove your details whenever you ask.

If we have a data breach that is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) as required, and tell any affected agency.

Access and correction

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by contacting us. We respond within 30 days. If we refuse a request, we tell you why in writing and how to complain.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how we have handled your information, contact us first. We acknowledge complaints promptly and aim to resolve them within 30 days.

If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.

Our website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc. in the United States. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses in its server logs for security purposes, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

  • We set no cookies and use no analytics or tracking.
  • Fonts and images load from our own site, not from third parties.
  • Our contact form and talent register form send your answers to us by email through Web3Forms, a form service run by Web3Creative from India. Web3Forms uses providers that may process data in the United States and other countries, and checks each sender’s email and IP address with spam filters. It keeps a copy of each submission, which we can see in our account with them, for up to three years unless we delete it sooner. The forms load nothing from Web3Forms and set no cookies.
