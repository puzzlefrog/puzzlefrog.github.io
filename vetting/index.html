Vetting

How we check people

Before anyone starts work with you, we make sure they fit the job and complete the checks below, and we give you the results. If they hold a security clearance, we help them keep it current for as long as they work with us.

How we judge fit

Our principal consultant, a senior business and systems analyst, assesses every candidate. A piece only fits when all four sides match, so we look at four things:

  1. 1

    Skills

    They can do the work, at the level you need. Our principal consultant talks through real work with every candidate, rather than matching keywords on a CV.

  2. 2

    Environment

    They know your kind of systems, methods and tools, and how work gets done in government.

  3. 3

    Security

    They pass the checks the job requires, and hold or can get the security clearance it needs.

  4. 4

    Team

    They communicate clearly, take direction well and work easily with your staff and other contractors.

Checks before they start

Everyone completes these checks before they start, whether we found them or you did.

  1. Police checkA national police check, considered against the job they will do.
  2. Medical or fitness checkA medical or fitness check that suits the job and the workplace.
  3. Australian citizenshipRequired unless you agree otherwise in writing.
  4. Security clearanceAny clearance the job needs, at the right level and valid on their first day.
  5. Other checks you ask forFor example an Employment Suitability Clearance or a working with vulnerable people check.
  6. Qualifications and skillsWe confirm their degrees, certificates and skills are genuine.
  7. Two employer referencesAt least two references from employers in the past five years.

For each check we collect the paperwork, get the person’s permission for you to see and keep copies, and answer any other questions you have about whether they are suitable.

What you receive about each person

We only put someone forward if they have agreed to it. For each person, you receive:

  • identity and contact details
  • relevant qualifications, training and licences
  • work history, including previous employers
  • references
  • our notes on how they fit the job
  • confirmation that each check is complete, with the results
  • any clearances they hold or, if none, whether they are willing to apply for one
  • proposed start and finish dates, and the rate

Security clearances

Government security clearances are granted by the Australian Government Security Vetting Agency (AGSVA). Each clearance must be sponsored, meaning a government organisation applies for it and backs it. For our workers, that is normally the organisation they are working in.

  • Already cleared. We check the clearance level and renewal date with the person, and your security team confirms it with AGSVA before they start.
  • Needs a new or higher clearance. Your organisation sponsors the application. We help the person fill it in completely and accurately, which avoids delays.
  • While they work with you. We remind them of their duties as clearance holders, such as reporting changes in their personal circumstances to AGSVA and to you, and we keep track of renewal dates.
  • When the job ends. We tell you straight away, so the clearance can be transferred or closed.

Our candidates hold clearances up to highest level, e.g. NV1.

We describe clearance capability at company level only. ASIO has warned that publishing a person’s clearance makes them a target for foreign intelligence services, so we never publish the clearances of individual workers.

While they work with you

  • They sign any confidentiality agreement you require, and we make sure they understand it.
  • They follow your security policies, including the Protective Security Policy Framework (PSPF) and Essential Eight where your contract requires them.
  • We check in regularly with you and with them, and act quickly on any concern.
  • If they can’t continue, we help you find a replacement.

How we treat our workers

At PuzzleFrog we look after our workers and are fully transparent with them about pay, career growth and opportunity.

  • People know what they will be paid before they agree to be put forward for a job.
  • We never charge candidates a fee to find them work.
  • Everyone who works for us has a direct contact at PuzzleFrog for the whole job.

Missing a piece in your team?

Tell us the job, level, clearance and start date. We reply within response time, e.g. one business day.
