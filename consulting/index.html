Consulting

Some IT problems are puzzles. Our principal consultant, a senior business and systems analyst, works them out with you. We prefer to contract with government directly, rather than through a larger firm.

Puzzles we solve

Six kinds of IT problem we’re asked to work out, in the words clients use, and what we do about each one.

01

“We’re not sure exactly what the new system has to do.”

Requirements gathering

Working out, with the people who will use a new system, what it must do, then writing that down clearly for the people who will build it.

02

“The way we work has grown complicated.”

Business process analysis

Looking closely at how work gets done today, and finding what could be simpler, faster or cheaper.

03

“Our system doesn’t fit the job any more.”

Systems analysis

Reviewing the systems you have now and recommending whether to improve them or replace them.

04

“The new software isn’t doing what we bought it for.”

Software implementation

Helping to set up and configure new software so it does the job it was bought for.

05

“Our system is hard to change and hard to grow.”

Application architecture

Designing how the parts of a system fit together, so it runs well, can grow and is easy to look after.

06

“Our systems don’t share information.”

System integration

Connecting separate systems so they can share information reliably.

How we work

Done by a senior analyst

The work is done by our principal consultant, who has more than 25 years in IT. You deal with the same person from start to finish. About our principal consultant

Direct to government

We prefer to contract directly with departments and agencies rather than through a larger firm. That gives better value, more flexibility and a clear view of who is doing the work.

Part of your team

We’re used to working inside government teams and fitting in with the way your people already work.

Plain communication

We explain technical matters in everyday language, so the people making a decision understand what they are deciding.

Tell us what you’re trying to achieve. We’ll tell you plainly whether we can help.
