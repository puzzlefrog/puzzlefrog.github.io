About us

About PuzzleFrog

An IT company set up in 2018. We supply IT professionals who fit into government teams, and we solve IT problems ourselves.

Leadership

Led by a senior business and systems analyst

Our principal consultant is a business and systems analyst with more than 25 years in IT. The work centres on working out what a system needs to do, mapping how work really gets done, and designing how systems fit together. That includes work inside government departments alongside public servants and other contractors, and on large projects run by multinational IT companies.

Good at explaining technical detail to business people, and business needs to technical people.

  • Business analysis
  • Systems analysis
  • Requirements
  • Process mapping
  • Solution design
  • System integration

Why PuzzleFrog?

Every team is a puzzle, and so is most IT work. Our job is to find the piece that fits: the right person for your team, or the right answer to an IT problem.

A person fits when their skills, their experience of your kind of environment, their security checks and the way they work with others all match what you need. An answer fits when it solves the real problem, not just the one on paper.

Who we are

PuzzleFrog is a small, specialist IT company. We prefer to contract directly with government rather than through a larger firm, so you always know who is doing the work.

We find skilled, fully checked IT professionals to work in government teams, and our principal consultant takes on consulting work directly.
