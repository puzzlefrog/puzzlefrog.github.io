Work health and safety

Under work health and safety law, PuzzleFrog and the organisation where someone works are both responsible for keeping them safe. Before anyone starts, we:

  • check the workplace, any hazards and the induction plan with you
  • tell the person who to contact about safety, both in your organisation and at PuzzleFrog
  • agree with you how incidents and concerns will be reported and handled

Our people can raise a safety concern with us at any time, and we work with you to fix it.

Modern slavery

The Modern Slavery Act 2018 (Cth) only requires companies earning over $100 million a year to report, so PuzzleFrog doesn’t have to. We still look for, and act on, any risk of modern slavery in our business and among our suppliers:

  • everyone who works for us has a written contract, is paid correctly and on time, and is free to leave
  • we never charge candidates fees or hold their identity documents
  • we check how any business we subcontract to treats its workers

If we find modern slavery anywhere in our business or among our suppliers, we will act straight away to stop it.

Indigenous procurement

We support the Commonwealth Indigenous Procurement Policy. We aim to buy more from Indigenous businesses, for example as subcontractors and suppliers, and to employ more Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people. We welcome job applications from Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people.

Workplace gender equality

Only employers with 100 or more staff must report under the Workplace Gender Equality Act 2012 (Cth), so PuzzleFrog doesn’t have to. We pay people doing the same job on the same basis, and we are open with everyone about their pay.

Cyber security

We protect the information our clients and job applicants trust us with, and we keep this website simple and safe:

  • it sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking, and loads nothing from other websites
  • anyone who finds a security problem can report it to us, using the contact in our security.txt file

Where a contract requires it, we meet your Protective Security Policy Framework (PSPF), Essential Eight and Information Security Manual (ISM) requirements, and show evidence that we do.
