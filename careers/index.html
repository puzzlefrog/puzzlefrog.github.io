Careers

Careers and talent pool

We help IT professionals find work in government teams. Join our talent pool (the list of people we contact about jobs) and we’ll get in touch when a job suits your skills, clearance and availability.

Jobs we fill

Mostly IT jobs, such as:

  • Business Analyst
  • Systems Analyst
  • Solution Architect and Application Architect
  • Systems Integration and Deployment Officer
  • Software Engineer
  • Test Analyst

Most roles are in locations, e.g. Canberra. See the full list of roles.

Before you apply

Most government roles require Australian citizenship, and many need a security clearance or the ability to get one.

Please don’t publish your clearance level on public profiles. Tell us privately instead.

How to apply

  1. Send us your CV

    Email your CV to info@puzzlefrog.com.au with “Talent pool” in the subject, or use our contact form. Tell us the roles you’re interested in, your availability, where you can work, and whether you hold a government security clearance.

  2. We talk with you

    We acknowledge every application within timeframe. Our principal consultant, a senior business and systems analyst, then talks with you about the work you’ve done and the work you want.

  3. We tell you about suitable jobs

    When a suitable job comes up, we explain it, including the pay, and ask your permission before putting you forward. We never put you forward without your agreement.

  4. We complete the checks

    If you’re chosen for a job, we complete the checks the government requires: police, medical or fitness, citizenship, clearances, qualifications and references. What the checks involve

What we collect and why

We collect your CV, contact details, work history, qualifications, referee details and clearance status so we can work out which jobs suit you and, with your permission, put you forward for them.

If you’re chosen for a job, we also collect the results of your checks, and the details we need to pay you.

Our privacy policy explains how we store, use and share your information, and how to see or correct it.

Our promise to workers

At PuzzleFrog we look after our workers and are fully transparent with them about pay, career growth and opportunity.

We never charge candidates a fee to find them work.
