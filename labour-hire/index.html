Fit

IT professionals for your team

Skilled IT people who fit into your team and work under your direction, for as long as you need them. Everyone is checked before they start.

Three ways to work with us

The difference is who finds the person, and who employs them. Whichever you choose, we supply people at APS 5 to EL 2 equivalent levels.

01

We find them. We employ them.

Seller-sourced labour hire workers

We advertise the job, search our contacts, interview people and check them, then send you a shortlist. The person you pick is employed by PuzzleFrog (or contracted to us) and works in your team for the agreed time.

02

You find them. We employ them.

Buyer-sourced labour hire workers

You’ve already found the person you want. We employ them, complete the checks, and handle their pay, insurance and paperwork while they work in your team.

03

We find them. You employ them.

Seller-sourced buyer workers

We find and check people for a job in your organisation. The person you choose becomes your employee, not ours.

Assessed by an analyst, not a keyword search

Every candidate we put forward is assessed by our principal consultant, a senior business and systems analyst.

We talk through real work they have done, ask how they would handle situations in your environment, and listen to how they explain technical detail. It’s the conversation a senior colleague would have with them, and it tells us far more than a CV.

We only put someone forward when all four sides of the fit are right.

The four sides of a good fit

  1. 1

    Skills

    They can do the work, at the level you need. Our principal consultant talks through real work with every candidate, rather than matching keywords on a CV.

  2. 2

    Environment

    They know your kind of systems, methods and tools, and how work gets done in government.

  3. 3

    Security

    They pass the checks the job requires, and hold or can get the security clearance it needs.

  4. 4

    Team

    They communicate clearly, take direction well and work easily with your staff and other contractors.

Security clearances

Many government jobs need a security clearance. When we put someone forward, we tell you privately which clearance they hold.

If a job needs a clearance the person doesn’t have yet, your organisation applies for it on their behalf (this is called sponsoring), and we help them through the process.

How clearances are sponsored and kept current

Why we don’t publish clearances

ASIO has warned that publishing a person’s clearance makes them a target for foreign intelligence services. So we never publish our workers’ clearances, or the clearance levels our candidates hold.

How it works, from request to first day

What happens after you ask us for someone.

  1. You tell us what you need

    The job, level, clearance, location, start date and how long you need them for. Before going further, we check that we can genuinely help and that there’s no conflict of interest.

  2. We find people

    We search our talent pool and contacts, and advertise if needed. Our principal consultant assesses each person against the four sides of a good fit.

  3. We check them

    We complete the checks and get each person’s written permission before we put them forward. How we check people

  4. You get a shortlist

    Each person’s CV, qualifications, references, check results and availability, our notes on their fit, and a clear breakdown of the price.

  5. You interview

    You interview the people you want to meet. We arrange the times and pass on your feedback.

  6. They start, and we stay in touch

    We handle the contract, pay, superannuation and timesheets, check in regularly with you and the person, and arrange an extension or a replacement if needed.

Already found someone?

If you’ve chosen the person yourself, we skip the search. We get their written consent, employ them, and look after their pay and paperwork. We complete the checks too, if you ask us to.

Missing a piece in your team?

Tell us the job, level, clearance and start date. We’ll tell you plainly whether we can help.
